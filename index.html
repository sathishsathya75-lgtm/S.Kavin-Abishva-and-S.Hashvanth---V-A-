How to use

  • Camera effect: Allow camera permission, then tap any spot to place the model on your screen. Rotate and zoom it. This is a screen-aligned camera overlay, not real table tracking.
  • True table AR: Needs a supported Android phone with ARCore, Chrome and WebXR hit-test. Move camera over a table until the ring appears, then tap Place.
  • 3D: Works without camera. One finger rotates, two fingers pinch, and +/− buttons zoom. Use Move Moon to control eclipses.
  • All modes: The student names stay in a small floating credit that moves away during zoom. Their original photographs are embedded directly into the publicly hosted HTML. Solar/Lunar choose an alignment. Press Play orbit only when you want motion. Use Earth/Sun/Moon buttons for a close-up.

Scientific note: Sizes and distances are not to scale; eclipse shadows and lunar reddening are illustrative. Sun surface is a detailed computer-generated solar texture; Earth/Moon are mapped with astronomical imagery when loaded.

Camera footage remains on your phone and is not uploaded by this webpage.
